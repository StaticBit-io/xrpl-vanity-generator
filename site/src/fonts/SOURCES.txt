The Latin file covers this range only: U+0000-00FF, U+0131, U+0152-0153, U+02BB-02BC, U+02C6, U+02DA,
U+02DC, U+0304, U+0308, U+0329, U+2000-206F, U+20AC, U+2122, U+2191, U+2193, U+2212, U+2215, U+FEFF, U+FFFD.
A character outside it, such as an arrow to the right or an approximately-equal sign, would fall back
to another face, so the pages do not use one. The theme rules out box-drawing glyphs for the same reason.
